Accessibility at the foundation

Property services designed for more people

The platform combines voice interaction, multilingual content, keyboard navigation and clear visual guidance. Users can always switch back to ordinary text and form controls.

Keyboard accessible

Navigate forms, menus and controls without requiring a mouse.

Screen-reader support

Semantic headings, visible labels and status messages help assistive technologies explain each action.

Voice and text choice

Voice assistance remains optional, with text input available as a permanent alternative.

Readable presentation

Text resizing, focus indicators and high-contrast controls support different visual needs.

🔐

Privacy and responsible AI

Sensitive property information requires strong protection

Smart Property AI is designed around controlled access, data minimization, secure document delivery, audit records and human review of important decisions.

  • Role-based access for landlords, tenants and staff
  • Protected documents and property records
  • Human review for legal and financial decisions
  • No automatic training from unreviewed conversations
  • Permission checks before sensitive actions
